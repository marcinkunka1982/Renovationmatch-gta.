Privacy information — draft

RenovationMatch GTA collects project details, city, contact name, email and telephone to respond to renovation requests and, where authorized, share them with one suitable contractor. Contractor applicants provide business and contact information.

Requests are stored in a database controlled by the site operator. Do not submit sensitive information. This demonstration has not yet implemented a production retention schedule, deletion request process or verified privacy contact. It is not ready to accept real personal data.

Before launch, the operator must publish a verified privacy contact, retention and deletion schedule, service provider disclosures, and a reviewed consent statement compliant with applicable Canadian privacy requirements.
